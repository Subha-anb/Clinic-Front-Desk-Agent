The orange dashed line is the escalation path: clinical questions become staff tickets.

Patients talk to one front-desk service. It checks every message, answers from clinic documents, and books through the MCP server into Google Calendar and Sheets. Claude only ever sees redacted text. n8n triggers the follow-ups, and clinical questions go to staff as tickets.
