Data protection

Patient data is protected by design

WhatHow it is protected
Phone numbersReplaced by a coded ID; never sent to Claude, the logs or n8n
Personal detailsRemoved from every message before it reaches Claude
AppointmentsPatients can only see, cancel or move their own
Calendar entriesShow the doctor and a short reference, no names
Incoming messagesEach one is checked to really come from WhatsApp
ConsentReplying STOP turns off all follow-up messages
Only the restricted staff sheet holds phone numbers. Everything else, including the AI, works with a coded ID. The AI also cannot be tricked into acting on another patient's appointments, because the system, not the AI, decides who the patient is.
