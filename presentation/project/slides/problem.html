The problem

Small clinics lose patients at the front desk

Missed calls

Patients who can't get through book somewhere else.

Repeat questions

Staff answer the same timing, fee and preparation questions all day.

Forgotten follow-ups

No reminders means no-shows. No check-ins means no reviews.

Three leaks at the front desk: unanswered calls, staff time spent on repeat questions, and follow-ups nobody has time for. Each one costs patients or revenue.
