The solution

One WhatsApp assistant covers four jobs

Answers questions

From the clinic's own FAQs, doctor schedules and pre-visit instructions

Books appointments

Into Google Calendar, with every visit logged in Google Sheets

Follows up automatically

Day-before reminders, post-visit check-ins and review requests

Escalates to staff

Anything clinical goes to the nursing team with a ticket

The assistant handles the routine work end to end: questions, bookings and follow-ups. The fourth job matters most: it knows when to step aside and hand over to a person.
