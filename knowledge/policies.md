# Clinic policies

## Cancellation and rescheduling
You can cancel or reschedule free of charge up to 2 hours before your appointment, on WhatsApp or by calling the front desk. Late cancellations and no-shows are not charged, but after three no-shows we may ask you to come as a walk-in.

## Late arrival
If you are more than 15 minutes late, we may need to move you to the next available slot.

## Privacy
We only discuss appointments, reports and records with the patient themselves. Parents or guardians can manage appointments for children under 18. For anyone else, the patient must give written authorisation at the front desk. We never share your information with third parties for marketing.

## Reminders and messages
We send a reminder the day before your appointment, a check-in message the day after your visit, and occasionally a request to review us. Reply STOP at any time to stop these messages.

## Accompanying persons
One person may accompany each patient. Children under 18 must be accompanied by a parent or guardian.
