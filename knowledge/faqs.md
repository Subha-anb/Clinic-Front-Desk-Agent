# Clinic FAQs

## Opening hours
We are open Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM. We are closed on Sundays and public holidays. The diagnostic lab opens earlier, at 7:30 AM, for fasting blood tests.

## Location and parking
Sunrise Family Clinic is at 12 Lake View Road, Adyar, Chennai 600020, next to the Adyar bus depot. Free parking for about 15 cars is available in the basement; enter from the side lane. Two-wheeler parking is at the front.

## Pharmacy
There is a pharmacy on the ground floor, open the same hours as the clinic. You can fill prescriptions from any doctor there.

## Consultation fees
A general consultation is Rs. 500. A specialist consultation (paediatrics, diabetes and endocrinology) is Rs. 800. A follow-up visit within 7 days of the first visit for the same issue is free. Lab and imaging charges depend on the test; the front desk can give you a quote.

## Payment and insurance
We accept cash, UPI, and all major debit and credit cards. We are empanelled with Star Health, HDFC ERGO, ICICI Lombard and Niva Bupa for cashless lab and imaging. Please bring your insurance card and a photo ID.

## What to bring to your first visit
Bring a photo ID, your insurance card if you have one, any previous prescriptions and test reports, and a list of the medicines you currently take. Please arrive 10 minutes early to complete registration.

## Walk-ins
Walk-ins are welcome, but patients with appointments are seen first. Booking an appointment on WhatsApp is the fastest way to be seen.

## Test reports
Most blood test reports are ready the same evening. Ultrasound and X-ray reports are ready within 24 hours. Reports are sent to your registered WhatsApp number as a password-protected PDF and can also be collected at the front desk with a photo ID. Reports are only released to the patient, or to someone with the patient's written authorisation.

## Children
Dr. Karthik Iyer sees children (paediatrics). A parent or guardian must accompany patients under 18.

## Languages
Our staff speak English, Tamil and Hindi.

## Medical questions
This WhatsApp assistant only handles appointments and clinic information. Questions about symptoms, medicines, test results or treatment are passed to our nursing team, who reply on this chat within 2 hours during clinic hours. In an emergency, call 112 or 108 immediately.
