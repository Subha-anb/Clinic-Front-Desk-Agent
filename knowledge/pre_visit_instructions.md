# Pre-visit instructions

These are the clinic's standard preparation instructions. If you take regular medicines, are pregnant or breastfeeding, have diabetes, or care for a child having a test, do not change anything on your own: message us and our nurse will give you personal instructions.

## Fasting blood tests (sugar, lipid profile)
Do not eat or drink anything except plain water for 10 to 12 hours before the test. Plain water is allowed and encouraged. No tea, coffee, juice or chewing gum. Come to the lab between 7:30 AM and 10:00 AM. The test takes about 10 minutes.

## Non-fasting blood tests
No preparation is needed. You can eat and drink normally.

## Urine test
Collect a mid-stream sample in the sterile container from the lab. Early morning samples are preferred. Hand it in within 1 hour of collection.

## Abdominal ultrasound
Do not eat for 6 hours before the scan. You may drink plain water. Wear loose, comfortable clothing. The scan takes about 20 to 30 minutes.

## Pelvic ultrasound
Drink 4 to 5 glasses of water one hour before the scan and do not empty your bladder until after the scan. The scan takes about 20 minutes.

## X-ray
No fasting is needed. Remove metal jewellery, including rings, chains and earrings, from the area being X-rayed; you can wear them again straight after. Tell the technician if there is any chance you might be pregnant. An X-ray takes about 10 minutes.

## Vaccination visits
Bring the vaccination card. For children, bring the child's vaccination booklet. Plan to stay 15 minutes after the vaccine so staff can observe.

## Consultation visits
Bring previous prescriptions, reports and a list of current medicines. Arrive 10 minutes early.
