Clinic Front Desk Agent architecture

A WhatsApp assistant that handles logistics for a small clinic and sends anything clinical to staff. This page shows how a message moves through the system, where the guardrails sit, and who can see patient data.

System overview

What happens to one message

  1. Identify. The phone number becomes a keyed hash (patient_ref). Rate limit and length cap apply.
  2. Rules. Patterns catch emergencies, medicines (including brands and misspellings), doses, symptoms with advice-seeking, result questions, other people's records and prompt injection.
  3. Classifier. Claude labels the redacted text. The stricter of the two verdicts wins. A refusal counts as clinical, and an API error keeps the rules verdict.
  4. Route. One of six outcomes. Emergencies caught by the rules skip the classifier, so the 112 / 108 reply goes out with no model call.
  5. Output guard. Model-written replies are blocked if they contain a dose, a medicine name, reassurance or PII that isn't in the clinic's own documents.
  6. Audit and reply. The decision and its reasons are logged with personal details removed, then the reply is sent.
